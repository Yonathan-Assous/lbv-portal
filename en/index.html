About us

Turning dreams into reality

La Belle Vie is a family business. For 15 years we have been organizing holiday stays and events for a traditional Jewish public, in Hebrew, French and English.

    A fundraising call center

    Before the evening, volunteers call donors from their own phones, and everything is recorded in the system.

    1. Upload the list The donor list from Excel, in one click.
    2. “Next call” Each volunteer gets the next person in line. Two volunteers never call the same person.
    3. Log the result No answer, call back, pledged, refused, already donated or link sent.
    4. A payment link Whoever pledges gets a link, and the donation lands on the dashboard.

    People who didn’t answer go back into the queue automatically, and those who asked for a callback get called at the time they chose.

    What to know about us

    Before choosing a producer, it helps to know what we do best, and who we are less suited for.

    Our strengths

    • Personal care from the owner. Ilan is on site at every stay and every event, not just at the first meeting.
    • Everything under one roof. Production, a raffle and donations system and an RSVP app, without juggling five suppliers.
    • Body and soul together. Mehadrin kashrut, classes and rabbis, alongside shows and relaxation.
    • Hebrew, French and English. The team speaks to every family in its own language.
    • 15 years of experience with holiday stays and events for a traditional Jewish public.

    Who we are less suited for

    • People looking for the cheapest option. We don’t compete on the lowest price, but on an experience where you don’t have to worry about a thing.
    • Last-minute bookers. Places are limited, and over the holidays they go fast.
    • Anyone wanting a non-kosher event. All our events are strictly kosher.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is included in the price of a stay?

    Accommodation, all meals with mehadrin kashrut, classes, evening shows and the kids’ club. Before you book you receive a full breakdown of what is and isn’t included, so there are no surprises.

    Is there a sukkah for each family?

    Meals are served in the hotel’s main sukkah. In balcony and garden rooms, a private sukkah can also be set up next to the room, depending on its layout.

    Can we come for only part of the stay?

    It depends on the stay and availability. Send us the dates that suit you and we will check.

    How far ahead should we book event production?

    For a wedding, contact us a few months ahead, especially in peak season. For a bar mitzvah or gala, shorter notice can work, depending on availability.

    Can we take only the gala system, without production?

    Yes. You can rent just the raffle, ticketing and donations system, and we come to run it on the night.

    Can we use the wedding app without full production?

    Yes. The RSVP and seating app works on its own.

    Do you work with English and French speaking families?

    Of course. Many of our guests come from abroad, and the team speaks Hebrew, French and English.
